What a price is built from

Every quote comes down to the same handful of things. Knowing them helps you tell whether any quote, ours or anyone else's, is reasonable.

  • Size and layout. Square footage, number of bathrooms, number of floors and whether there are stairs.
  • Condition. A maintained home and a home that has not been cleaned in a year are different jobs, and pretending otherwise is how surprise invoices happen.
  • Occupied or empty. Empty units clean faster but get judged harder, because there is no furniture hiding anything.
  • Scope. Inside the oven, inside the fridge, inside cabinets, windows, balcony glass: each one adds time, so each one is priced, not assumed.
  • Access. Elevator bookings, loading docks, parking and walk-up distance all cost time on cleanouts and removals.
  • Volume. On junk removal and cleanouts, price follows how much goes in the truck and what it costs to dispose of properly.

Recurring work costs less per visit

A property cleaned every two weeks never gets far from clean, so each visit takes less time. Weekly, biweekly and monthly schedules are priced per visit and come in under a comparable one off clean. Commercial and landlord accounts with several properties are quoted as a block.
