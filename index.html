What you can count on

Plain commitments, no fine print.

1A price before we startNo hourly creep

You approve the quote first. If we find more than the photos showed, we tell you and agree the change before carrying on.

2We finish the jobIncluding the awkward part

Behind the fridge, the top of the frames, the balcony glass. We'd rather stay the extra twenty minutes than leave it obvious.

3Walk it with usBefore we pack up

If you're on site, we walk the space together at the end. If you're not, you get photos. Anything missed gets put right.

4Early starts, seven days7am weekdays

Trades at nine, cleaners at seven. We work around handover times, listing photos and tenant moves.

5Careful in an empty homeKeys, codes, concierge

Plenty of our work happens with nobody there. We lock up, report in and leave the place exactly as we found it, minus the mess.

6Donate and recycle firstOn cleanouts

Usable furniture and goods go to a charity or a reuse centre where we can. Metal and electronics go to recycling, not landfill.
